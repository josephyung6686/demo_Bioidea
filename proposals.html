Obtimize pipeline: design proposals

Structure is locked: one vertical flow, Ingest → Understand → Diagnose → Optimize → Ask, joined by a single line with short notes explaining each hand-off. Flat white cards. Below are three designs for each step. Pick one letter per step (for example "1B, 2B, 3A, 4C, 5B, connector A") and I will build exactly that.
