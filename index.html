An AI brain for your lab, in three layers.

Memory and optimization are one system. The memory produces the clean data the optimizer needs. The optimizer gives your team a reason to keep the memory complete.

Storage

Connects read-only to the drives and instrument folders you already use. Originals stay untouched, so every number traces back to its source.

  • FCS
  • Microscopy
  • Plate reader
  • Protocols

Memory

Each experiment is rebuilt as one connected record: conditions, cell line, passage, protocol version, operator, outcome and source files. Software estimates stay marked as drafts until a scientist signs them.

  • CHIR
  • BMP4
  • VEGF
  • Coating dose
  • Passage
  • Draft vs. signed

Optimization

Recommends the next batch of experiments within a fixed well budget. It returns a reliable operating range instead of one fragile recipe, and it tells you when the data can't support a decision yet.
