Why we started Obtimize

This summer we were building an optimization system for iPSC to endothelial cell differentiation at NutriGene AI, a biotech company in Hong Kong.

The lab sent us around 300 instrument files covering three different experiments, all mixed together. We pulled out 31 data points. Not one was ready for optimization.

Before AI can help scientists design better experiments, it has to understand what the lab has already done.

What Obtimize does

A lab-wide second brain that understands experiments, not just documents. It keeps everything your lab knows in one connected memory and puts it to work.

How we handle your data

Read-only connections

Obtimize never writes to your drives or instruments.

Originals preserved

Every source file is kept unchanged and checksummed, so every number traces back.

Draft vs. signed

Software estimates stay drafts until a scientist verifies them.

Every claim cited

Answers link to the runs and files they came from. No invented data.

Abstains when unsure

If the data can't support a decision, it says so and asks for replicates.

Knows what can be compared

Flags mismatched assay versions, batch effects and wells sharing one plate, instead of silently pooling them.
