The problem

Labs lose what they learn

Every experiment leaves files behind: flow cytometry, microscope photos, spreadsheets, protocols. They pile up in shared drives and people's heads. Failed runs are forgotten, and old ones get repeated by accident.

What we saw

300 files, nothing usable

This summer at NutriGene AI in Hong Kong, we built AI to improve how stem cells are turned into blood vessel cells. The lab's history came as 300 files from 3 experiments, mixed together. None of the 31 data points was ready to use.

What Obtimize is

One memory for the whole lab

Obtimize reads your lab's files, links every experiment to its conditions, cells and results, and flags gaps or errors before they cost you a run.

What it does for you

It tells you what to run next

Using optimization methods we built and benchmarked, it recommends the next experiments, and says plainly when the data is not good enough yet.

How it works

Before AI can help scientists design better experiments, it has to understand what the lab has already done.

Built for small cell therapy teams without a data team. General AI tools can't read a flow cytometry file or tell a passage 12 run from a passage 18 one. Obtimize can. Here is how, in four steps.

03 · Optimize

An optimization engine built for stem cell manufacturing

Built for the realities of differentiation: few wells per round, noisy readouts and line-to-line variation. It plans your next plate, finds a range that holds, and lets you question every run.

The most informative wells

Chosen to teach you the most per round within a fixed well budget, not repeats of the best run.

A range, not a point

Conditions that hold across run-to-run noise, which is what matters at scale-up.

Honest when unsure
